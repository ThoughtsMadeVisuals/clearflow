Money Tracker

Pick how you paid (cash, a credit card or an e-wallet) under "Paid with" when you log spending, bills or company expenses. Everything already counts in your monthly totals. Credit cards also show what you owe and when it's due, so don't log the card payment itself as an expense.

Backup and spreadsheet sync

Your data is stored only on this device. Download a full backup now and then, and use it to move your data to another phone or computer.

Export CSVs that match the Google Sheet tabs, or import rows from them. Imports add new rows and skip duplicates.
